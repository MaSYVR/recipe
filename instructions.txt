A GARLIC BREAD RECIPE FOR THE AGES
1) Cut the delicious sourdough bread you made in half widthways
2) Put plenty of butter in a bowl in the microwave, crush the garlic in it and turn the microwave on for 30 seconds
3) Stir the bowl and spread over the cut pieces of bread
4) Sprinkle over some oregano, salt and pepper
5) Broil for 1-2 mins, keeping an eye on it to make sure it doesn't burn
6) Eat it
